Work

I’ve built software for nearly twenty years and led engineering teams for six of them. Most of that work sits where customers meet the product: messaging platforms, help desks and contact centers, and now the AI arriving inside them. I do my best work close to customers, and I write the guides and sample code that help them succeed.
